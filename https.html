What is HTTPS?

HTTPS stands for HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure. It protects communication between users and websites.

What is SSL/TLS?

SSL/TLS certificates encrypt data sent over the internet, preventing attackers from intercepting sensitive information.

Why It Matters

HTTPS helps ensure confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of online communications.
