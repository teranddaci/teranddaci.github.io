Common Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails and websites designed to steal information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that damages systems and steals data.

🔒 Ransomware

Encrypts files and demands payment for recovery.

🎭 Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing confidential information.

Protect Yourself

Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords with letters, numbers and symbols.

Two-Factor Authentication

Add an additional layer of protection to your accounts.

Updates

Keep devices updated to patch security vulnerabilities.

Safe Browsing

Avoid suspicious websites and unknown downloads.

HTTPS & SSL/TLS

HTTPS encrypts communication between users and websites. SSL/TLS certificates help ensure confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of online communications.
