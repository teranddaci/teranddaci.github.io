Why Cybersecurity Matters

Cybersecurity has become essential in today's digital world. Individuals, businesses, and governments rely on secure systems to protect sensitive information from unauthorized access, cybercrime, and data breaches.


Understanding common cyber threats and following safe online practices can significantly reduce the risk of becoming a victim of cyberattacks.

Common Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages, and websites designed to steal passwords, banking information, and personal data.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that damages systems, spies on users, or steals confidential information.

🔒 Ransomware

A type of malware that encrypts files and demands payment for recovery.

🎭 Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information through deception and trust.

How to Protect Yourself

Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords using uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

Two-Factor Authentication

Add an additional security layer that protects accounts even if passwords are compromised.

Software Updates

Regular updates help patch vulnerabilities and keep systems protected from emerging threats.

Safe Browsing

Avoid suspicious websites, downloads, and links from unknown sources.

HTTPS & SSL/TLS Certificates

HTTPS (HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure) is the secure version of HTTP used for communication between web browsers and websites.


SSL/TLS certificates verify website identity and encrypt transmitted information, helping prevent interception by attackers.


HTTPS provides confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity. Users can identify secure websites by the padlock icon shown in modern web browsers.

Cybersecurity Best Practices

Use Unique Passwords

Avoid using the same password across multiple accounts.

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication significantly increases account security.

Verify Websites

Always check for HTTPS and trusted domain names before entering information.

Keep Software Updated

Updates often contain important security fixes and patches.
