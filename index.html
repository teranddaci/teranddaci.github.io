About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and personal information from digital attacks. As our lives become more connected, understanding cybersecurity becomes increasingly important.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing emails and fake websites
  • Malware and ransomware attacks
  • Password theft
  • Identity fraud
  • Social engineering attacks

How to Protect Yourself

  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Keep software updated
  • Avoid suspicious links and downloads
  • Use HTTPS-secured websites
